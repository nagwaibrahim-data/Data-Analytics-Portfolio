# 💊 Pharmacy Inventory Analysis — Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes pharmacy inventory data to support better stock management and inventory-related business decisions.

The dashboard provides a clear view of inventory value, stock levels, product status, capital tied up in inventory, and products that may require management action.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective is to answer key inventory management questions such as:

- How much capital is currently tied up in inventory?
- Which products are low in stock?
- Which products are out of stock?
- Which products are overstocked?
- Which products require reordering?
- Which products represent the highest inventory value?
- How is inventory distributed across ABC classes?
- Where should management focus its inventory decisions?

## 📈 Dashboard Structure

The dashboard includes analytical pages designed to support different inventory management decisions.

### 🏠 Overview

Provides a high-level view of inventory performance, including inventory value, stock status, product quantities, and products requiring attention.

### 🚨 Inventory Action

Focuses on products that require management action, including low-stock, out-of-stock, and overstocked products.

### 📊 ABC & Capital Analysis

Uses **ABC/Pareto analysis** to classify products based on their contribution to inventory value and identify where the largest amount of capital is concentrated.

---

## 🔍 Inventory Analysis

The analysis focuses on:

### 📦 Stock Status

Products are analyzed based on their current stock condition, such as:

- Healthy Stock
- Low Stock
- Out of Stock
- Overstock

### 💰 Inventory Value

Inventory value is analyzed to identify products that represent a significant amount of tied-up capital.

### 🔄 Reorder Analysis

The dashboard identifies products that may require replenishment based on their stock levels and reorder requirements.

### 📊 ABC / Pareto Analysis

Products are classified into **A, B, and C categories** based on their contribution to total inventory value.

This helps management prioritize high-value inventory items.

---

## 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard helps management:

- Identify products requiring immediate attention.
- Monitor inventory levels.
- Detect excess inventory.
- Identify capital-intensive products.
- Prioritize high-value products using ABC analysis.
- Support more informed purchasing and replenishment decisions.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, management can:

1. Monitor low-stock and out-of-stock products regularly.
2. Prioritize replenishment for products requiring reorder.
3. Review overstocked products to reduce unnecessary capital tied up in inventory.
4. Focus inventory control efforts on high-value **A-class products**.
5. Monitor inventory value and stock status continuously.
6. Use ABC analysis to support purchasing and inventory planning.
